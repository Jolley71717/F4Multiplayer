The campaign, together

How much of Fallout 4 two people can play together from start to finish, quest by quest. "Shared" is what the mod is built to do: whoever moves the quest on moves everyone on. "Played together" is only what has actually been played through with two games on our test rig, so it is the smaller number, and it grows as test runs happen.

The host's settings

This page is for the host's default settings: the story shared (sStory = shared) and More quests off (sQuests = story). With More quests on in the host's F1 menu, 20 more of the quests below are shared (they are marked More quests: shared): most of Far Harbor's town quests and Nuka-World's park and later raider quests. With the story set to each player's own (sStory = own), the main quest and the factions' quests are each player's own, and the side quests stay shared.
