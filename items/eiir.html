Host options to make being downed and revived harder

Shipped in 0.9.0; the next release also tells joiners the host's rules. Waiting for the long test.

Mergedstep 6 of 8

Players asked for ways to keep side-by-side play from making everyone invincible. 0.9.0 added host settings for how long a downed player bleeds out, how long a revive takes and the health it gives, how many revives per fight or per time window, a cooldown, and whether helpers can revive during a fight. The next release also tells a joiner the host's rules when they join, and a rule change waits until a downed player is back up.
