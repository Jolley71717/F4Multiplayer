Borrow a friend's parked power armor frame

Written behind the owner's setting bLendPowerArmor; waiting to be built, reviewed and tried on two real games.

Builtstep 3 of 8

Players asked to borrow a friend's parked power armor. The plan is that the frame stays its owner's: a friend climbs in, walks off and fights in it, and when they climb out it is parked again as the owner's. Open questions include who takes armor damage and fusion core drain, and what happens if the borrower parks it somewhere else. Until then 0.9.2 shows a 'coming in a later version' message.
