A friend's shots look aimed at you

Partly fixed in 0.9.2; we haven't checked the aim while firing in a real game.

Looked intostep 2 of 8

A player said that when a friend shoots, it looks as if they are shooting at you. 0.9.2 fixed the most likely cause: a friend's character fired real shots at your character. Their shots now follow where they actually aim. We have not checked the aim during firing in both directions.
