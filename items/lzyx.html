Explosions from enemies you can't see hit you (DLC you don't own)

Fixed for the next release; blasts thrown by enemies aren't tested yet.

Mergedstep 6 of 8

If the host has a DLC you don't own, its enemies are not shown in your game, but their explosions could still hit you. The next release names the enemy that set off a blast, so a game without a copy of that enemy doesn't set the blast off. Players' grenades were tested both ways on two real games.
