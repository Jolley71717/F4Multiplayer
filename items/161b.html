Power armor frames duplicate and appear in odd places in Sanctuary

Fixed in 0.9.2, with a clean-up for the next release; waiting for the long test before it ships.

Mergedstep 6 of 8

On 0.9.1, power armor frames kept duplicating and turning up in random places around Sanctuary. 0.9.2 stopped a load from keeping the copies of friends' frames. The next release also stops a frame moved in workshop mode from being shared as a building, and removes the copies older versions made that way. Extra frames already in your world from 0.9.1 can be removed in the console: click one, then type disable and markfordelete.
