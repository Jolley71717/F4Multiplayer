See a friend raise their Pip-Boy and a marker when in a menu

The raised wrist and name tag shipped in 0.9.0; the icon above the head is still off by default.

Queuedstep 1 of 8

Players asked to see a friend's Pip-Boy, the raise animation, and an icon over their head while they are in a menu. Since 0.9.0 a friend in the Pip-Boy raises their wrist, and their name tag says [Pip-Boy] or [in a menu]. The icon exists but is off by default (bMenuIcons) because it only drew partly. Showing the Pip-Boy itself is tracked separately.
