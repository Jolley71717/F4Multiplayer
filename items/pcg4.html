Doors and gates opened by scripts stay shut for friends (Vault 114)

Merged for the next release; script-locked doors tested in two games; Vault 114 itself not yet.

Shippedstep 8 of 8

Vault 114's cog door opened for the host but stayed shut for a friend, because a script opens it. 0.9.0 shares Vault 114's gear door and gates opened from terminals and card readers, and 0.9.2 shares chained doors, quietly scripted doors, two-state gates and doors NPCs open. These passed two-game tests, but the exact Vault 114 door was not tested and one of its two parts may not be covered. Doors that also start scenes or move NPCs stay each player's own.

  7. Long playtest · In a test build of the release, being played for several hours straight on two games.
