Friends appear out of power armor after the game was quit in it

Partly fixed in 0.9.2; not yet tried in a real game.

Looked intostep 2 of 8

If players quit while in power armor and start a session later, others saw them without a suit until they got out and back in. 0.9.2 fixes the sending side, so a player who loads a save in power armor is seen in it. We have not tried it in a real game, and there may be other reasons a frame is missing on the receiving side.
