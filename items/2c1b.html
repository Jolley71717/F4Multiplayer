See a friend's Pip-Boy and a marker when they are in a menu

Built but held up: the Pip-Boy model we hang on the friend's character doesn't show yet, so it stays off by default.

Reviewedstep 4 of 8

Players asked to see a friend's Pip-Boy on their wrist, the tapping animation, and a marker above their head. Since 0.9.0 a friend in the Pip-Boy raises their wrist and their name tag says so. We built a Pip-Boy that hangs on the friend's character, off by default behind bMenuBadges, but in testing the model we picked does not appear and we need a different one.
