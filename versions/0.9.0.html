F4Multiplayer · released 2026-10-20

Version 0.9.0

18 new, 19 fixed.

Shipped2026-10-20

Install it over 0.8.11 or any 0.9.0 test build. (This is the same file as the 0.9.0-rc3 test build, so seeing "rc3" in your log is normal.) Friends with different mods can now play together, items are given by dropping them, the host has kick, ban, lock and password controls, friends move much more smoothly, and loot, plants, radiant quests and security gates are shared. It was run through the full two-game test set and a multi-hour soak with no crash.

Everyone needs the same build to play together: it uses protocol 49, so a friend on 0.8.11 or 0.9.0-rc1 gets "Version mismatch" and has to update too (0.9.0-rc2 and rc3 play with it). A saved session from 0.8.11 or older still loads.

New

  • Feature

    Different mods, or the same mods in another order, can play together. Every form the games talk about is now numbered by the session, so load orders no longer have to match. What only one game has is skipped, never guessed: the joiner is told which mods only one side has, and the rest plays normally.

  • Feature

    Give each other items by dropping them. Drop a weapon, armor, aid or junk and your friend sees it on the ground; whoever presses Take first gets it, and it exists in one place only, through saves and reloads on either side. Keys, notes, quest items and items made at runtime are refused, and the giver is told. Putting a weapon or armor in a container shows a hint to drop it instead.

  • Feature

    Host controls in the F1 menu: kick, ban (kept across restarts), lock the session, and make up or remove a password. A dedicated server has the same commands at its console (help lists them).

  • Feature

    Where is everyone: F10 (iKeyWhere) prints each friend's distance and direction on the HUD ("Sam: 240 m NE").

  • Feature

    Better pings: F8 pings what your crosshair is on (an NPC, an item, a friend or a spot), puts a ring on everyone's compass in your color for 15 s, and names it with its distance and direction.

  • Feature

    Shared radiant quests: when one of you is handed a repeatable job whose targets are the game's own places and people, everyone gets the same quest for the same settlement, place and target, and each player is paid once. That covers most of the Minutemen's settlement requests, the Brotherhood's (BoSR01, BoSR02, BoSR05), the Institute's (InstR01, InstR02, InstR05), the Railroad's (RRR01 to RRR06, with the HQ's job count kept right) and Diamond City's wanted posters. bSharedRadiants turns it off.

  • Feature

    Smoother friends: each state is stamped with the engine frame it was read in, so a friend's character moves about two-thirds less unevenly on every connection, and carries on through a late update instead of freezing and leaping. How far behind a friend is drawn adapts to the connection: on a good link about 100 to 150 ms instead of 50, more on a worse one, and it shrinks again when the link recovers. After a join, friends are no longer drawn 1.5 s behind.

  • Feature

    A friend in their Pip-Boy raises their wrist on their stand-in, and their name tag says "[Pip-Boy]" or "[in a menu]".

  • Feature

    Session rules the host sets: how long a downed player bleeds out, how long a revive takes and the health it gives, how many revives per fight or per window, a cooldown after getting up, and whether helpers can revive during a fight (iBleedOutSeconds, fReviveSeconds, iReviveHealth, iMaxRevives, iReviveWindowMinutes, iReviveCooldownSeconds, bReviveOutOfCombat). The HUD says how many revives are left.

  • Feature

    VATS in real time (bRealTimeVats, on by default): your VATS no longer slows the game for your friends. Jet still slows time as it does alone.

  • Feature

    Tutorial hints are off during a session (bHideTutorials) and come back afterwards.

  • Feature

    Linux and Steam Deck: the log says whether the game runs under Proton, whether Steam started it, why Steam isn't ready if it isn't, and how the hotkeys are read. INSTALL.md has a Linux section on starting F4SE through Steam. Accepting a friend's invite while you're hosting now tells you to set bHost = false, instead of doing nothing.

  • Feature

    Security gates opened from terminals, card readers and buttons open for everyone, including for a friend who comes later. Vault 114's gear door (Valentine's quest) now opens for everyone too.

  • Feature

    Settlement building: power wires, building done before the session started, and pieces the settlement came with that you move now show up for your friend. Each game shows the power its builder's game reports, and a friend's copies can't be wired, stored or scrapped by mistake, so no settlement grid keeps a dead reference after the session.

  • Feature

    Objects you pick up and move by hand (a bottle, a tire), drop or throw show up moved for your friend, and things you knock over land in the same place for a friend nearby.

  • Feature

    A friend's LooksMenu tattoos, face and body paint and skin override show on their stand-in, when both games have LooksMenu and the overlay mods.

  • Feature

    A friend's grenade, mine or Molotov shows in their stand-in's hand through the throw (bGrenadeInHand).

  • Feature

    Muzzle flash on every replayed shot of friends and mirrored NPCs.

Fixed

  • Bug

    The black screen on joining: the vault's own quest stages (Vault 111's pod, lift and mirror scenes) are no longer shared, since every player goes through the vault on their own path, and a fade a quest left up is faded back in.

  • Bug

    A Molotov's fire, or a friend's grenade, no longer heals an NPC back or hits it twice: the NPC's own game shares its health whenever it changes, and the others report the damage they dealt.

  • Bug

    Crashes taking from some containers and using terminals (Red Rocket's desk among them) are hardened against, and a friend's looting of a container you aren't near waits until you come back.

  • Bug

    "You cannot use this at this time" on a power armor frame that leans, or whose seat the game still thought was yours.

  • Bug

    A chest or corpse a friend looted bare is bare in your game too, even though each game rolls its own loot. Weapons, armor, notes, keys and quest items stay each player's own, as before.

  • Bug

    A plant a friend picks is picked in your game too, and two players picking the same plant harvest it once.

  • Bug

    Dogmeat's copy runs instead of squatting along at a run's pace.

  • Bug

    Damage counts on enemies again after loading a save, and no ghost enemies after a reload.

  • Bug

    Alt-tabbing out of the game no longer makes your character fire or move at clicks and keys meant for another window.

  • Bug

    A downed player the game stood back up (in a pile of clutter, or after a save) is put down again and keeps waiting for help.

  • Bug

    No emotes in power armor (they slid the view sideways there), with a line saying why.

  • Bug

    A friend's gun is in one piece on their stand-in after they swap it for another copy of the same gun.

  • Bug

    Friend markers and pings show on the power armor compass too.

  • Bug

    A friend in power armor walks in it, instead of sliding along still or popping in and out of view without it.

  • Bug

    A downed friend's stand-in lies down until they're helped up, instead of standing.

  • Bug

    A crash on saving (manual, quick or auto) while a friend's stand-in was touching you.

  • Bug

    With a mod plugin installed, loading a quicksave (or any save made after the mod was added) could take the save for another character: the host restarted the session and friends had to rejoin, or a friend left it. The session file for your character is kept and renamed the first time you host with this version.

  • Bug

    Taking only a weapon, armor, a key or a note from a chest or corpse, when nothing else shared is left in it, leaves it bare for friends too; before, their copy kept its own junk.

  • Bug

    Settlement beds and containers left mid-move when workshop mode closes are drawn where they really stand, and crafted containers that come stocked (a bureau) reach your friend, emptied.

Known issues

  • LooksMenu overlays follow LooksMenu's published source and haven't been tried with LooksMenu installed yet; reports welcome.
  • Only Vault 114's gear door and the plain terminal and card-reader gates are shared. Doors whose scripts also start scenes or move NPCs stay each player's own (docs/script-doors-survey.md lists them).
  • Radiant quests whose enemies or helpers are made on the spot stay each player's own: settlement attacks and the Castle's defense, rescues and escorts (MinRecruit08/09, BoSR03/04, InstR03/04, RRR07 to RRR11), random-encounter quests and the DLCs' radiants.
  • The Pip-Boy and other menus still pause your own game.
  • The Linux notes in the log haven't been tried on Linux yet; logs from Linux players are welcome.
