F4Multiplayer · released 2026-11-04

Version 0.11.2

3 fixed.

Shipped2026-11-04

A patch for 0.11.1: a crash when enemies hit a friend whose character was just redrawn, a friend's NPC lines that could keep playing as noise, and quest enemies that existed in only one game. Install it over 0.11.1 or 0.11.0. (This is the same file as the 0.11.2-rc1 test build, so seeing "rc1" in your log is normal.) It uses the same protocol (57), so it plays with 0.11.1 and 0.11.0, and a session saved by them loads as it is. It is best if everyone in a session has it.

Fixed

  • Bug

    A game could crash when an enemy's hit on a friend's character arrived just after that character's stand-in was replaced (seen with Nuka-World raiders firing at a friend). The game no longer runs that hit's reaction (the victim turning to fight back) on a stand-in that is already gone; the hit itself still lands. We couldn't make the crash happen on our test machines, before or after the fix, so this guards against the cause the crash log showed.

  • Bug

    A friend's NPC line played aloud in your game now stops when its recording ends, and at most four play at once. Before, a line's sound was stopped only when the same speaker spoke again, so a recording that never ended on its own kept playing and each new speaker added to it. One player heard a loud noise grow with every friend's line; this may be its cause, but we haven't confirmed it on their setup.

  • Bug

    An enemy that a friend's game switched on through a quest (the mirelurks at Barney Rook's bunker in Gun Run) now comes on in your game too. Before, it existed only in the friend's game: you couldn't see it, and they fought something nobody else saw. Enemies and characters that a later quest stage switches off again are left to your own story, and with Shared story off (the host's setting in the F1 menu) nothing changes.

Known issues

  • When both players are near a friendly NPC, the NPC still runs in one game only, and in the other it may not greet, idle or work (talking to it works).
  • A friend's lights light in your game only once that friend has been to the settlement in this session.
  • A joiner caught up past Dangerous Minds misses the companion's talk after the surgery: that stage is skipped because it would move the joiner into Kellogg's memory.
  • Settler jobs aren't shared: each game keeps its own. A friend sees the settler go to work but not the job.
  • Storing a pre-placed settlement crop during a session removes it from the host's world but not the friend's.
  • Mines were tested indoors with frag mines; other mine types and outdoor mines haven't been tested yet.
  • Pieces built before 0.11.0 aren't covered by the fix for built pieces a builder's save no longer has: one that is already stuck in a friend's game stays.
  • A late joiner still sees an enemy a friend's game spawned (not one from the world) at full health until it is next hurt.
  • An item from the world picked up before you joined, or before your last load, is not shared when you drop it.
  • Players whose mods change the leveled lists differently can still see different enemies.
  • Misc quests are still each player's own: only the enemies a friend's stage switches on are shared. Gun Run's first mirelurks and the Easy City Downs robot race are still each player's own.
  • Quest enemies are shared this way only when no mod changes them or their quest marker; with an unofficial patch or a previs mod that does, they can still exist in one game only.

Reporting a problem: the bug form, with the plugin log (Documents\My Games\Fallout4\F4SE\F4Multiplayer.log, or F4Multiplayer.1.log if you restarted the game since) and, after a crash, the crash log from Buffout 4 or Addictol. The plugin log names players as player N and leaves Steam ids and addresses out; crash logs and screenshots can still hold names.
