F4Multiplayer · released 2026-11-03

Version 0.11.1

1 new, 12 fixed, 2 changes.

Shipped2026-11-03

A patch for 0.11.0: friendly NPCs that stood silent in a friend's game when the other player was off elsewhere, a friend's lights that stayed dark, crafting that ignored the settlement's junk, and a batch of smaller fixes. Install it over 0.11.0. (This is the same file as the 0.11.1-rc1 test build, so seeing "rc1" in your log is normal.) It uses the same protocol (57), so it plays with 0.11.0 and a session saved by 0.11.0 loads as it is. It is best if everyone in a session has it, since several of the fixes need both games to carry them.

New

  • Feature

    A friend's NPC lines now show in the game's own subtitle area, timed with the speech, instead of short notices at the top left that were gone before you could read them. This needs General Subtitles on in the game's settings; with it off, or while you are in a conversation yourself, you still get the notices.

Fixed

  • Bug

    Fewer friendly NPCs standing in silence in a friend's game. An NPC runs its own AI in one game only, and until now it went to the nearer player only once the other was very far away, so one player often found town NPCs that never greeted or turned to them. A friendly NPC now goes to the game of a friend standing next to it when you are well away (about 2000 units). An NPC your game takes over also drops the do-nothing behaviour its copy had and goes about its own business.

  • Bug

    Crafting at a station a friend built now counts the junk stored in your settlement's workbench, not only what you carry.

  • Bug

    A friend's lights now light in your game once they are powered. Many of the workshop's lights (the construction light, the light box, the high-tech lights, the strobe, the fan light and the mirror ball) stayed dark when they were already powered as they appeared. A friend's light bulb now also casts its light.

  • Bug

    A friend's built pieces stay tracked when their cell unloads and loads again: their power, scrapping and moving keep working, and leaving a session cleans them up. Before, a piece in a cell you had left could be lost track of, and a second copy made over it.

  • Bug

    Dropping an item you picked up from the world (a stimpak lying in Sanctuary, say) is now shared: your friend sees it land.

  • Bug

    A killing blow of yours on a friend's NPC now reaches the game that runs it, so a Mr Gutsy no longer stays alive on a sliver of health in its own game after a long fall.

  • Bug

    Two games loading the same new outdoor area at once could build an enemy from different templates. Both now read the area's level zone itself, not the game's copy of it, which isn't set yet while the area loads.

  • Bug

    Catching up a friend's quests no longer leaves a menu open in a joiner's game: the settlement list a stage opens by itself (Getting a Clue, when Nick becomes the new companion) and the item inspection some stages open are closed for you.

  • Bug

    Side quests that the game starts with targets it picks for each player (robot assaults, Far Harbor and Nuka-World settlement and gang jobs, Saugus Ironworks, Weston Water Treatment) are no longer shared, so a friend's stage doesn't start your copy with your own targets. In the Automatron and Nuka-World quests, a friend's stage no longer moves you when you aren't with them, or takes caps or items from you.

  • Bug

    Getting into a power armor frame is safer: a rare chance of reading the game's data at the wrong moment is gone.

  • Bug

    After being downed and helped up, the downed camera and the input lock are now ended if the game left them on. A player saw this as a camera stuck underground and a Pip-Boy that wouldn't open, after a grenade both knocked them over and downed them. We couldn't make it happen on our test machines, so tell us if you still see it.

  • Bug

    The install guide says what to check when a Steam invite arrives but joining does nothing.

Known issues

  • When both players are near a friendly NPC, the NPC still runs in one game only, and in the other it may not greet, idle or work (talking to it works).
  • A friend's lights light in your game only once that friend has been to the settlement in this session.
  • A joiner caught up past Dangerous Minds misses the companion's talk after the surgery: that stage is skipped because it would move the joiner into Kellogg's memory.
  • Settler jobs aren't shared: each game keeps its own. A friend sees the settler go to work but not the job.
  • Storing a pre-placed settlement crop during a session removes it from the host's world but not the friend's.
  • Mines were tested indoors with frag mines; other mine types and outdoor mines haven't been tested yet.
  • Pieces built before 0.11.0 aren't covered by the fix for built pieces a builder's save no longer has: one that is already stuck in a friend's game stays.
  • A late joiner still sees an enemy a friend's game spawned (not one from the world) at full health until it is next hurt.
  • An item from the world picked up before you joined, or before your last load, is not shared when you drop it.
  • Players whose mods change the leveled lists differently can still see different enemies.

Reporting a problem

  • Install a crash logger (Buffout 4 or Addictol), or a crash leaves nothing to read.
  • Report on GitHub with the plugin log (Documents\My Games\Fallout4\F4SE\F4Multiplayer.log, or F4Multiplayer.1.log if you restarted the game since) and the crash log. The plugin log names players as player N and leaves Steam ids and addresses out; crash logs and screenshots can still hold names.
